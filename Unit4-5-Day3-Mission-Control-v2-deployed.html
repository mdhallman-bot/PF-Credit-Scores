Connect the Dots

Credit behavior → credit history → credit report → credit score / creditworthiness → lending decisions and terms

Credit history is the record. A credit report organizes that information. A credit score is a number created from report information to predict risk. Creditworthiness is the lender's broader judgment of risk.

Mission 5 — Brisk Investigation: Something Is Wrong With My Credit

YOUR MISSION: INVESTIGATE THE CREDIT CASE
Something is wrong with Taylor Morgan’s credit report—but not everything that looks suspicious is actually an error or fraud.

Case File — Taylor Morgan, age 20

  1. A creditor called Metro Retail Bank appears on the report, but Taylor does not recognize that name.
  2. A late payment from three years ago appears on the report. Taylor wants it removed because it hurts.
  3. Canyon Credit Union shows an account as OPEN, but Taylor believes the account was closed.
  4. Frontier Card Services shows a recently opened account that Taylor says they never opened.
  5. Taylor received an offer that says: “Pay $499 and we can erase all negative credit information.”
Your job in Brisk
  • Investigate at least 3 of the 5 findings.
  • Ask questions to uncover information that is not yet visible.
  • For each finding, determine: What is happening? What evidence supports your conclusion? What should Taylor do next?
  • Do not assume something is fraud just because it looks unfamiliar.

Start in Brisk by choosing one numbered finding and telling Brisk what you want to find out.

Save This for Real Life

Get your report

Use the federally authorized source.

AnnualCreditReport.com
Check it

Look for unfamiliar accounts, wrong payment status, incorrect balances/limits, closed accounts shown open, duplicates, and identity errors.

Fix errors

Dispute inaccurate information with the reporting company and the company that supplied it. Keep supporting records.

CFPB dispute guide
Protect your credit

A credit freeze is free and does not hurt your score. A fraud alert tells creditors to take identity-verification steps.
